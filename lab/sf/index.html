Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Still have questions? Book a free call with a Start Fund specialist and we’ll walk through your idea for a fund.

How do Start Fund and VC Lab fit together?

One application covers both. Start Fund is the fund itself: an institutional-grade venture fund you can launch in under a day. VC Lab is our free 14-week accelerator, with real deadlines and weekly feedback to get you to a first close fast. Plan on 20 to 30 hours a week during the program. You can launch a Start Fund even if you don’t join VC Lab.

What happens after I apply?

We review your answers. If you’re a fit for VC Lab, admissions invites you to a 15-minute call about your thesis and fundraising plan. To launch your Start Fund, you pass KYC and AML checks, and your fund and bank account are ready in under a business day.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront, and VC Lab is free with no equity. A Start Fund follows the 2 and 20 model: you earn a 1% management fee and 18% carried interest. We receive a 1% operations fee and 2% carried interest, which cover fund formation, administration, compliance and tax preparation.

How does the $10K first check work?

It’s a catalytic investment we make into select Start Funds. It works more like a sponsorship than a typical LP investment, and it counts toward your fund’s commitments until your first close. Not every Start Fund receives one, so ask our team about yours.

What’s my role in a Start Fund?

You’re the Investment Lead. You raise from LPs, source deals, track the portfolio, and handle LP communications. Because Decile Group is the official General Partner handling legal, compliance, and back-office ops, you can’t call yourself General Partner or Managing Partner.

Who decides on investments?

An Investment Committee makes the final call on every deal. When you submit a deal memo and supporting docs, the committee reviews it, and you get a decision in three days or less. Most deals go through unless there are extreme terms, unusual legal or compliance risks, or conflicts of interest.

Where can I launch a Start Fund?

All Start Funds are domiciled in Delaware, and a few limits apply. A Start Fund can’t accept LPs from Europe, the UK, India, or Japan. Investment Leads can’t live in India or Singapore. And Start Funds can’t invest in Indian startups or in crypto.

Does a Start Fund count as real track record?

Yes. It’s a real venture fund with institutional-grade operations, and it’s fully portable. When you’re ready, you can set up your own general partner and management company and move the fund over, or launch a Fund II at any time.
