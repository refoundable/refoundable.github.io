Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Still have questions? Book a free call with a Start Fund specialist and we’ll walk through your idea for a fund.

Is this my VC Lab application?

Yes. The questions at the top of this page are your application to VC Lab Cohort 23. When you hit Apply, your answers go to our admissions team.

What happens after I apply?

Admissions reviews your plan. If it’s a good fit, we’ll invite you to book a call and find the cohort that suits your timeline. Apply by Dec 14 for Cohort 23, which starts Jan 14.

Why is Decile my first LP?

It’s a catalytic investment: Decile commits $10K to your fund as your first LP. We do it because a first commitment gets funds to their first close faster, and that’s what we’re here for. Terms apply.

Is Start Fund really free?

Applying, VC Lab and setting up your fund are all free, with no fees and no equity. You only pay when you choose to run your fund’s operations with us.

Do I need venture capital experience?

No. Half of the managers who launch through VC Lab have no prior VC experience. What matters is a clear focus and a network of people who trust you.

How fast can I launch?

You can plan your fund in minutes, and launch an institutional-grade fund in under a day. Raising from LPs takes longer, and our team helps you at every step.

Who is Decile?

Decile Group helps people start, run and grow venture firms. More than 1,000 firms have launched through VC Lab, our free training program, since 2020.
