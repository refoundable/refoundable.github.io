Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Is Start Fund really free?

Yes. Setting up your fund is free. Decile earns revenue when you choose to run your fund’s operations with us.

What is the $10K, exactly?

It’s a catalytic investment: Decile commits $10K to your fund as one of your first LPs. To claim it, set up your fund in Hub, add your first potential LPs, and send us a PACT, Decile’s short letter of intent. Terms apply.

Do I need venture capital experience?

No. Half of the managers who launch through VC Lab have no prior VC experience. What matters is a clear focus and a network of people who trust you.

Can I start a fund on the side?

Yes. You set the pace. Hub keeps your plan, your LPs, and your documents in one place, so you can pick up where you left off between meetings.

How fast can I launch?

You can plan your fund in minutes. With Start Fund, you can launch an institutional-grade fund in under a day. Raising from LPs takes longer, and Hub helps you at every step.

Who is Decile?

Decile Group helps people start, run, and grow venture firms. More than 1,000 firms have launched through VC Lab, our free training program, since 2020.
